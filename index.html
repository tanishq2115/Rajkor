The Snapchat Connection

Everything kicked off with pure comedy. Her little sister Didu sparked the curiosity, telling Rajkor that I was talking to her. Rajkor got all excited and sent a Snapchat friend request. After a brief dynamic where the request was cancelled and sent right back, a silent connection was built. We both sat there for 3 or 4 days without saying a single word.

Breaking Ice Over Laughter

The ice finally broke when I sent a random snap of Mayur getting completely scolded. She instantly replied, asking if I also called him by his nickname, and just like that, the endless talking began. Fast forward to June 28th—her birthday. I messaged her, and she spent the evening talking, sharing the start of her day with me despite her party happening all around her.

Voice Notes and Growth

The whole month of July went incredibly well. A unique dynamic formed: she would constantly send voice messages, and because I wasn't fond of the sound of my own voice, I stuck strictly to text bubbles. Eventually, I sent her a short voice note. Even through rough patches, misunderstandings, and days of complete silence later in the month, we managed to find our footing again.

Reconnections & Continuous Conversations

After being completely offline for a stretch of 20 days, destiny brought us right back around early September when she reached out on Instagram. Apologies were made, things healed, and mid-September brought us our very first voice call. Ever since, regular calls became our thing. Mostly, she just talks and talks, and I genuinely love just sitting back and listening to her endless updates.

Shared Humour & Navigating Obstacles

October brought its own share of panic when family structural boundaries came into play, leaving us worried we wouldn't be able to stay in touch. Luckily, the tension dissolved quickly. We shifted into a fun phase of constantly sharing relatable, lighthearted couple reels back and forth, navigating her natural mood swings with complete patience and mutual understanding.

To My Absolute Favourite Human...

Regardless of our chaotic mood swings, the minor disagreements, the brief moments of silence, and the crazy misunderstandings we run into... I want you to know something clearly.

"I never want to lose you, and I want to create a lifetime of amazing memories with you."

Whether it's laughing at inside jokes, sharing updates throughout the day, or waiting to hear from you the exact moment you get back from school or tuition... you are irreplaceable to me. Thank you for all the laughter, the stability, and the pure joy you bring into my life. Let's keep making this story longer.
